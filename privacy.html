Privacy Policy — DIIIIIIID Monitor

Last updated: 2026-10-15

What data is accessed

With the account owner's consent, the app uses the Gmail read-only scope (gmail.readonly) to read emails in the owner's own Gmail accounts, and the Google Sheets read-only scope to read the owner's own spreadsheets.

How data is used

Only order-confirmation and shipping emails are processed to extract order number, item, quantity, amount and tracking number. This information is used solely to keep the owner's own purchase records and to send status notifications to the owner's private channel.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored locally on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, and it is not used for advertising. The app never sends, deletes or modifies email.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
